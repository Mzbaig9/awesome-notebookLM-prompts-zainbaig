# Operation Handwriting, Plan

Dar Al-Ulum Montreal. 10 school days, 60 minutes per day, same slot daily. Suggested dates: Monday 21 September to Friday 2 October 2026.

Groups: Cycle 1 (grades 1 and 2), Cycle 2 (grades 3 and 4), Cycle 3 (grades 5 and 6).

Scripts: Cycle 1 print. Cycle 2 print repair (Days 1 and 2) then cursive. Cycle 3 cursive fluency, speed, presentation. Lead teacher moves a group one track up or down after the Day 1 diagnostic if needed.

Language: worksheets are letter-based and work in English and French. Test passage exists in both. Each student tests in the language of their main writing class.

## 1. Target

Day 1 and Day 10: same test passage, scored on the 24-point rubric.
Target: average gain of 6 points per cycle, no student under 12 on Day 10.
Under 12 on Day 10: four weeks of 10 minutes daily in homeroom, then re-test.

## 2. The hour

| Minutes | Block | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | Warm-up | Posture check, grip check, warm-up card |
| 5 to 15 | Direct teaching | Teacher models on the board, says the pathway. Air writing, desk writing, one on paper |
| 15 to 35 | Guided practice | Worksheet of the day. Teacher circulates: posture, grip, formation |
| 35 to 50 | Applied writing | Sentence, paragraph or dictation on lined paper |
| 50 to 57 | Feedback | Student circles best letter. Teacher: one star, one wish |
| 57 to 60 | Tracker | Progress chart, sheets into folder |

The 60 minutes are not shortened or split.

## 4. Roles

Principal: launch message, walk-through Days 1, 5 and 10, receives scores Day 10.
Lead teacher per cycle: runs the hour, scores tests, keeps tracking sheet, hands in scores Day 10.
All subject teachers: illegible work is handed back to be rewritten, every class, both weeks.
Assistants: guided practice block, five weakest students per group, separate table.

## 5. Materials per group

Print before Day 1: folder per student, pre-test and post-test sheets, daily worksheets for the cycle, 20 sheets of the cycle's lined paper per student, progress chart per student, tracking sheet and rubric per teacher, warm-up card and posture poster, certificate per student.

Physical: HB pencils, erasers, triangular pencil grips for fist or thumb-wrap grips, slant board or thick binder for heavy pressers. No pens for Cycles 1 and 2.

## 6. Data

Day 1: pre-test scored same day, entered on tracking sheet.
Day 5: students not moving are marked, they get the assistant table from Day 6.
Day 10: post-test scored, entered, average gain per group. Sheet to principal with the under-12 list.

Report to principal per cycle: average pre-test, average post-test, number under 12.

## 7. After

Subject teachers keep returning illegible work. Homerooms keep 5 minutes of copying at the start of the first writing period for the rest of the term. Under-12 list: 10 minutes daily with lead teacher for four weeks, re-test.

Arabic script is not covered. Same structure, own letter families, separate module.
