Culture has its place
04 / 10

"Were your people not so new to Islam, I would have rebuilt the Kaaba on the foundations of Ibrahim."

To Aisha after the conquest. Sahih al-Bukhari

He had the authority. He had the truth. He still left the Kaaba as the people knew it, because changing it would have shaken them. Idols he destroyed. Attachment he respected.

Islam did not come to destroy how we live. It came to tame how we see things. Urf has its place in a marriage too.

At home
05 / 10
كان بشرا من البشر
He was a man among men. Aisha, describing him at home

In the masjid, the imam. On the battlefield, the commander. Before a delegation, its head. At home, a man like any other.

He mended his own garment. He fixed his own sandals. He was in the service of his family. And when the adhan was called, he left for salah.

No takalluf. Ease was his default. Salah was the boundary.

Sahih al-Bukhari

On rights
07 / 10

Fulfil yours.
Ask Allah
for yours.

Marriage is a majmua of rights, a bundle of what two people owe each other. His principle for every right, from rulers to spouses: give what is owed by you, and ask Allah for what is owed to you.

He never took revenge for himself. When Abu Bakr walked in on Aisha raising her voice at him and moved to intervene, it was the Prophet ﷺ who stepped in between and calmed it down.

Sahih al-Bukhari. Musnad Ahmad

Boundaries
08 / 10

It was not
a fairy tale

His marriage had tension. The honey plot between Aisha and Hafsa, which had him swear off honey until Allah corrected him. The request for more provision that went against the very temperament of Nubuwwah.

He went quiet. He withdrew for twenty nine days. Then Allah gave his wives a choice: the world, with a graceful release, or Allah and His Messenger. They chose him.

Easygoing by default. Principled when it counted.

Al-Tahrim 1. Al-Ahzab 28 to 29. Sahih al-Bukhari

"I know when you are pleased with me and when you are angry. When you are pleased, you swear by the Rabb of Muhammad. When you are angry, by the Rabb of Ibrahim."

To Aisha. Sahih al-Bukhari

When she struck down the plate of food sent by another wife, he picked up the pieces and said only, "Your mother got jealous."

He named the feeling. He did not punish it.

Mawaddah and Rahma
10 / 10

Love gets you started.
Rahma gets you home.

Passion takes a marriage to a point. Mercy, the benefit of the doubt, the patience to let go, takes it to the end. A car needs all four wheels.

Surah al-Rum 21
Affection within its channels
Serving your family
Giving the benefit of the doubt
Letting go of your own ego

And choose for deen. His khutbah of nikah never mentions nikah. It only mentions taqwa.

Save this. Send it to someone getting married.
